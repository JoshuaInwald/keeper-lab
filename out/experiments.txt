EXPERIMENT 2 — leave-one-season-out on the exchange rate
================================================================================================
 held_out  pooled_$/pt  actual_$/pt  mean_resid  rmse
     2022        6.533        4.155      -0.431 3.557
     2023        6.194        4.782      -0.039 3.246
     2024        5.659        6.357       0.457 2.977
     2025        5.525        8.092       0.181 3.250
     2026        5.415        9.981      -0.225 3.078

  mean residual on the held-out season runs -0.43 to +0.46 roto points
  corr(held-out year, mean residual) = +0.289  -- weak and NOT monotone,
  so a pooled fit is not systematically biased by season in the
  *residual*. What it does miss is the level: pooled $/pt sits at
  5.4-6.5 while the held-out season's own rate
  runs 4.2-10.0. Pooling gets the ranking right and the
  price level wrong, which is exactly why EXCHANGE_BASIS exists.

  observed closer excess: +2.23 roto points
  null distribution (label shuffled within season x price band):
    mean +0.217, sd 0.501, 95% range [-0.76, +1.20]
  two-sided p = 0.0000  (5000 permutations)
  The shuffle holds season and price band fixed, so this is not
  the time trend and not 'cheap players beat their price'.
