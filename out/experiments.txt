EXPERIMENT 2 — leave-one-season-out on the exchange rate
================================================================================================
 held_out  pooled_$/pt  actual_$/pt  mean_resid  rmse
     2022        5.960        3.823      -0.448 3.751
     2023        5.649        4.405      -0.056 3.460
     2024        5.184        5.783       0.470 3.172
     2025        5.064        7.275       0.198 3.422
     2026        4.954        9.085      -0.219 3.258

  mean residual on the held-out season runs -0.45 to +0.47 roto points
  corr(held-out year, mean residual) = +0.315  -- weak and NOT monotone,
  so a pooled fit is not systematically biased by season in the
  *residual*. What it does miss is the level: pooled $/pt sits at
  5.0-6.0 while the held-out season's own rate
  runs 3.8-9.1. Pooling gets the ranking right and the
  price level wrong, which is exactly why EXCHANGE_BASIS exists.

EXPERIMENT 3 — permutation test on the saves finding
================================================================================================
  observed closer excess: +1.87 roto points
  null distribution (label shuffled within season x price band):
    mean +0.229, sd 0.537, 95% range [-0.82, +1.32]
  two-sided p = 0.0006  (5000 permutations)
  The shuffle holds season and price band fixed, so this is not
  the time trend and not 'cheap players beat their price'.
