4. KEEP-VS-CASH: WHAT IS A DOLLAR OF AUCTION BUDGET WORTH?

  aggregate keeper surplus $473  ->  inflation 1.310
  so $1 of auction budget buys $0.76 of value, not $1.00

  This changes the keep rule. Cutting a player does not give you his
  value back -- it gives you his SALARY, which buys salary/inflation
  of production. So the correct test is:

      keep if   value  >  keeper_cost / inflation
